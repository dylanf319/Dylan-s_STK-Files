It is required to download the Addons within SuperTuxKart or on the website [SuperTuxKart Add-ons](https://online.supertuxkart.net/index.php) before replacing the custom Addons

To find out which kart, track, arena, or soccer field addons are modified, you can check the modified Kart and Track lists here.

- [Tracks](https://github.com/dylanf319/Dylan-s_STK-Files/blob/STK-1.5-rc1/supertuxkart/addons/tracks/Tracks%20Available.md) 📃
- [Karts](https://github.com/dylanf319/Dylan-s_STK-Files/blob/STK-1.5-rc1/supertuxkart/addons/karts/Karts%20Available.md) 📃

If you see that one or some addons are in their original appearance and not with the modification, you will have to reinstall the addons folder.
